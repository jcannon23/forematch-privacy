Help with ForeMatch for iPhone and Apple Watch

Contact us

Questions, bug reports or feature ideas: j.e.cannon5@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

How do I start a round?

On the Play tab, start a new round, pick a course (or enter the scorecard by hand), choose tees, add your players and pick your games. Players without the app can be added as guests by name.

How do my friends join?

Open the round and share its invite. Friends can scan the QR code with the ForeMatch app, tap the link, or type the join code. Once they join, scores and standings update live on every phone.

What happens if I lose signal on the course?

Scores are saved on your phone first and sync automatically when you're back online. Keep the app open or reopen it after you regain signal to make sure everything has synced.

Does ForeMatch handle money?

No. ForeMatch only calculates the results of side games your group agrees on. The optional “Pay with Venmo” button opens Venmo; any payment happens entirely in Venmo.

How are handicap strokes applied?

Each player's handicap is converted to a course handicap for the tees you choose, and strokes are given on holes by stroke index. If a course's details look wrong, email us the course name and tee so we can check it.

How do I use ForeMatch on Apple Watch?

Install the watch app from the Watch app on your iPhone. During a round, your watch shows the current hole and lets you follow along; the Live Activity also shows the round on your lock screen.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to the Profile tab and tap Delete Account. This removes your account and associated data. If you can't access the app, email us from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.
