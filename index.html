Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 30, 2026

ForeMatch (“we”, “us”, “the app”) helps golfers track matches, scores, and friendly wagers. This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible.

Information we collect

Account information. When you sign in with Google or Apple, we receive your name and email address to create and identify your account. If you use Sign in with Apple’s “Hide My Email,” we only receive the relay address Apple provides.

Round and scoring data. Rounds you create or join — including course, format, players, handicaps, hole-by-hole scores, leaderboards, and any wager/settle-up details (such as a Venmo username you choose to add) — are stored so the app can function and sync across the players in a round.

Course-search location (optional). If you grant location permission, your approximate location is used to suggest nearby golf courses. Location is used only at the moment you search and is not tracked in the background or stored by us.

Diagnostics. We use Google Firebase, which may collect basic app and device information (e.g., crash data and general usage) to keep the app reliable.

How we use information

  • To provide core features: accounts, scoring, leaderboards, and settle-up.
  • To sync a round among the people playing in it.
  • To suggest nearby courses when you search.
  • To maintain reliability and fix problems.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

How information is shared

  • With other players in your rounds. Your name, handicap, scores, and any Venmo handle you add are visible to the people in that round.
  • With service providers. We use Google Firebase (authentication and database) and Google/Apple sign-in, governed by their own privacy policies.
  • Payments. ForeMatch does not process payments. “Pay with Venmo” simply opens the Venmo app or website; any transaction happens entirely within Venmo.
  • Legal. We may disclose information if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active. You can delete a round you created, which removes its scores. You can permanently delete your account and its associated data at any time from within the app: go to Profile → Delete Account. This removes your profile, handicap, saved presets and notification settings; rounds you played remain with the other players in those rounds. You can also contact us at the email below if you need help.

Children

ForeMatch is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Security

We use reputable infrastructure (Google Firebase) and industry-standard transport encryption (HTTPS). No method of storage or transmission is 100% secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Changes

We may update this policy; we’ll revise the “Last updated” date above.
